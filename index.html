What this is

Clinical experience, translated into everyday decisions.

Diabetes advice can quickly become overwhelming: carbohydrates, HbA1c, weight, medication, protein, meal timing, activity, glucose readings and conflicting advice online.

This service is designed to turn all of that into a small number of sensible actions, with follow-up support to help you make them stick.
