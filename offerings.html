How I can help

Practical support, without turning food into a full-time job.

Different people need different levels of support. These are example offers for the prototype rather than final clinical packages or prices.

Single appointment

Metabolic Health Review

Illustrative price: £145

A 60–75 minute online consultation for someone who wants an experienced pair of eyes on the whole picture.

  • Current health, weight and diabetes history
  • Eating pattern and practical problem areas
  • Recent HbA1c / glucose information where available
  • Medication context and questions to take back to your prescriber when appropriate
  • A short, prioritised action plan after the appointment

Best for: someone newly told they have prediabetes or type 2 diabetes, or anyone who feels they have accumulated lots of advice but no clear plan.

12-week programme

Prediabetes Reset

Illustrative price: £495

A structured programme for people who want to act on a prediabetes result before it becomes a bigger problem.

  • Initial 75-minute assessment
  • Three follow-up appointments
  • Personalised nutrition and activity priorities
  • Support with weight loss where appropriate
  • Help understanding blood results and trends
  • End-of-programme maintenance plan

Best for: people who know they need to make changes but want something more individual than generic NHS leaflets or an app.

12-week programme

Living Well with Type 2

Illustrative price: £650

Support for adults with established type 2 diabetes who want to improve food choices, weight, confidence and understanding.

  • Initial assessment plus four follow-ups
  • Food, weight and glucose pattern review
  • Realistic goal setting
  • Support interpreting common diabetes terminology and measurements
  • Clear boundaries around when to involve the GP, diabetes team or prescriber

Best for: people who want more time and continuity than they currently get from routine appointments.

8–12 weeks

GLP-1 Nutrition Support

Illustrative price: £425

Nutrition support for adults using prescribed medicines such as Mounjaro or Wegovy.

  • Eating enough despite reduced appetite
  • Protein and muscle-preservation strategies
  • Meal structure and food quality
  • Practical approaches to common dietary side-effects
  • Planning for maintenance rather than relying on appetite suppression alone

Important: this is not a prescribing service. Medication initiation, dose changes and medical side-effects remain with the patient's prescriber.

A useful first version

Keep the service narrow, specialist and understandable.

The point of these offers is not to maximise the number of services. It is to make it immediately obvious who this is for and why specialist diabetes experience matters.
